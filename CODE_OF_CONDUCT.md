# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The airllm-gui project is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, disability, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from feedback.
- Respect privacy; do not publish personal information without consent.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement and reporting

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation for conduct they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub; do not post sensitive details in public issues.

This policy is based on the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
